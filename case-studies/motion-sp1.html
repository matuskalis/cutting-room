Market Reel

A performance comparison animated from real market data — two series drawn against each other over a decade, with the date advancing as the lines grow. Built as a template: change the tickers in a config file and the whole reel re-renders.

RoleDesign & build
ToolRemotion (React)
DataReal historical prices
Format1080×1920 vertical

Chart design at phone size

  • Two lines maximum. A comparison reel with four series is unreadable in vertical format. Two, each with its label riding at the end of its own line rather than in a legend.
  • The date is the clock. Large type under the chart advancing with the animation — it tells the viewer where they are without an axis they'd have to squint at.
  • Percentage axis, not price. Comparing two assets with wildly different absolute prices only works in relative terms, so the axis is percent change from a common start.
  • Curve, not candles. Smoothed lines rather than OHLC bars. At this size candles are visual noise.

Template, not a one-off

The reel is parameterised — series, date range, colours and labels all live in one config file, and the price history is fetched rather than hand-entered. That makes a second reel a five-minute job instead of a rebuild, which is the only way this format is worth doing more than once.

An animation exercise using publicly available historical price data. It is not investment advice and takes no position on either asset.
